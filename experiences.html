EXPERIENCE 01

Cultural & Historical Tourism

Uncover 5,000 years of civilization: the ancient brickwork of Bronze Age Mohenjo-daro, Gandharan Buddhist monastic universities at Taxila, the imperial splendours of Lahore Fort and Badshahi Mosque, and the glazed blue tile shrines of Multan.

Featured Places:
Lahore Walled City • Mohenjo-daro • Taxila Ruins • Makli Thatta • Multan

EXPERIENCE 02

Nature & Wildlife

Immerse yourself in exceptional biodiversity: the rolling 4,000m alpine wilderness of Deosai National Park protecting endangered Himalayan brown bears, the turquoise waters of Attabad Lake, and the otherworldly wind-carved desert rock towers of Hingol.

Featured Places:
Hunza Valley • Deosai National Park • Neelum Valley • Hingol National Park
Explore Nature →
EXPERIENCE 03

Mountains & Adventure

Legendary high-altitude expeditions: the gateway to K2 in Skardu, mountain jeep tracks to the foot of Nanga Parbat at Fairy Meadows, the jagged granite spires of the Passu Cones, and winter skiing at Malam Jabba in the Hindu Kush.

Featured Places:
Skardu & K2 • Fairy Meadows • Passu Cones • Malam Jabba Ski Resort
Explore Adventure →
EXPERIENCE 04

Sports & Festivals

Living community celebrations: traditional free-style polo matches played at 3,700m on Shandur Pass, the ancient polytheistic spring dances of the Kalash valleys, national folk crafts at Islamabad's Lok Mela, and intense desert jeep rallies in Cholistan.

Featured Events:
Shandur Polo • Chilam Joshi Kalash • Lok Mela • Cholistan Rally
View Festivals Guide →
EXPERIENCE 05

Lakes, Valleys & Countryside

Tranquil escapes and family retreats: lush pine forests and riverside wooden chalets in Kalam and Swat, cool morning hikes through the Margalla Hills of Islamabad, and trout fishing beside roaring Himalayan streams.

Featured Places:
Swat Valley • Islamabad Margallas • Mahodand Lake • Neelum Valley
Explore Valleys →
EXPERIENCE 06

Coastal & Marine Tourism

Pakistan's wild Arabian Sea coastline: the iconic hammerhead headlands and Omani history of Gwadar, dramatic desert sands meeting turquoise tides at Kund Malir, and scuba diving coral reefs at Astola Island.

Featured Places:
Gwadar Peninsula • Kund Malir Beach • Astola Island • Ormara Coast
